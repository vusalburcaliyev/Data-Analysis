Decisions that preserve the evidence
  • Raw source cell values are preserved in 15 compressed, checksum-locked extracts.
  • Damage suffixes are converted to dollars; explicit zero, missing, and invalid values have separate statuses. This snapshot has no missing or invalid damage tokens.
  • Outliers are flagged in the full table and removed only from the routine comparison view. No casualty record is deleted because its value is unusually large.
  • 1,269 zero-duration records stay visible. Nine durations longer than 72 hours are queued for review; the source does not justify a silent correction.
  • Event 970467 reports zero direct deaths in the event summary and two direct-fatality child records. Headline counts remain 389; the fatality-context analysis uses 391 direct records.
  • 58,578 linked location points cover 70.3% of events. Missing child points are not evidence that the details file has no coordinate.

What the records can and cannot establish

This is a descriptive analysis of NOAA-reported flash-flood records, not a complete census of every flood. Counts reflect reporting practices as well as weather impacts. County event records are not unique nationwide storms; episode IDs are reporting groups. Damage is estimated, nominal, and not equivalent to insurance claims, verified asset loss, or inflation-adjusted economic cost. Zero entries do not distinguish no damage from unquantified damage. No population, asset, rainfall, warning, or response-time exposure is available, so this report does not rank underlying hazard, measure warning effectiveness, or support causal claims.
